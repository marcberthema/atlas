Atlas UI exploration

Three visual worlds, three pages each, built from real Atlas content. Mocks only: nothing here is published. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so open them online.

A · Field guide

Entries are species accounts. Field marks (version, dates, licence) sit in a margin box, look-alikes get their own section, and the four families are colour-tabbed edge-index tabs on a cloth-green frame.
